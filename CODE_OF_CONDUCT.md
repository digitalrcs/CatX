# Community code of conduct

CatX welcomes contributors and users of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and avoid posting sensitive information.
- Give credit for others' work and honor artwork and software licenses.
- Help keep safety and reliable keyboard recovery central to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, publishing private information, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, lock conversations, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Reports will be reviewed as privately and fairly as practical.

This code applies in the repository and in public spaces where someone is representing CatX.
