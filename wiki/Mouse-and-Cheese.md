# One mouse. One piece of cheese. Plenty of cats.

Enable **Toy mouse visits** to add a shared mouse to the cats' world. There is one mouse for the group, regardless of how many cats you select.

## The cheese trip

1. The mouse leaves its hole and heads for visible cheese.
2. It picks up the cheese and carries it toward home.
3. Nearby cats can run after it.
4. The mouse tries to evade them. Some trips succeed; others end in a catch.

A successful delivery displays **+1 cheese**. A catch triggers a brief playful celebration before the next round.

## Natural play

The mouse travels across the screen to find cheese and changes course during a chase. Its evasiveness varies, giving both cats and mouse a chance to win. Its hole stays stationary during a trip.

Sleeping cats do not all abandon their naps for every visit. Let the scene run to see different combinations of roaming, sleeping, chasing, and deliveries.

## Control the activity

You can switch **Toy mouse visits** on or off while the cats are active. **Chase playful cursor** is a separate option, so either behavior can be enabled on its own.

Select at least one cat and enable the keyboard guard to see the scene.
