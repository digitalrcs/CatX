# Your first few minutes with CatX

1. [Install CatX](Installation) and open it.
2. Open **Choose your cats**. Click a name, then hold **Ctrl** and click additional names to select up to eight cats.
3. Click the dropdown arrow again to close the list.
4. Choose an **Unlock combination** you can comfortably press. Start with **Ctrl + Alt + K** if your keyboard has an awkward function row.
5. Leave **Auto-lock after no activity** set to **Off** while getting familiar with the app.
6. Select **Enable keyboard guard**. Your chosen cats appear.
7. Immediately test your chosen recovery shortcut to restore typing. Enable the guard again when you need it.

![CatX ready to enable the keyboard guard](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/digitalrcs/CatX/main/docs/images/main-window.png)

## Make it yours

- Turn **Chase playful cursor** on for reactions to quick changes in cursor direction.
- Turn **Toy mouse visits** on for a shared mouse that gathers cheese and tries to dodge the cats.
- Use **No cats / clear selection** for keyboard protection without cats.
- Minimize CatX to keep it in the notification area. Double-click its cat icon to reopen it.

The animations are click-through. Your mouse stays available while the keyboard is guarded.
