# Privacy

CatX runs locally and is designed to protect privacy.

- It requires no account.
- It makes no network requests.
- It collects no analytics or personal data.
- It does not translate, log, store, or transmit typed content.
- It does not record mouse positions or button names.
- It reads only Windows' last-input timestamp for the optional inactivity timer.
- Preferences are stored locally in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\CatX\settings.json`.

Read the repository's complete [privacy documentation](https://github.com/digitalrcs/CatX/blob/main/docs/PRIVACY.md).
